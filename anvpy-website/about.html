About AnvPy

Empowering developers to build the future of mobile applications

Our Vision

AnvPy was created with a simple yet ambitious vision: to make Python development on Android as powerful and accessible as on desktop platforms. We believe that mobile devices are not just consumption tools, but powerful development platforms waiting to be unlocked.

The Story

What started as an experiment to run Python on Android has evolved into a complete ecosystem for mobile development. AnvPy combines native performance with Python's simplicity, enabling developers to create everything from simple utilities to complex AI applications, games, and professional Android apps.

Today, AnvPy powers thousands of applications across the globe, from educational tools and productivity apps to cutting-edge machine learning projects and immersive games. Our community of developers continues to push the boundaries of what's possible with Python on Android.
